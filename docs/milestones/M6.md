# M6 — Breadth

Date: 2026-11-18. Commits: `4f59d35`, `c55e556`, and the CCXT commit (see `docs/PROGRESS.md`).

## What it delivers for a user

- **A check of a broker's trade file** (`/check`, and a panel on the dashboard). A user who trades in a broker app can choose a Kraken Trades CSV, or map the columns of any other broker's file (this is the only way to read a Coinbase file). Each fill is compared with the public price at its second. The page lists what it could not compare and why, and states its assumptions. Nothing is stored. The Kraken reading comes from Kraken's documentation and is labelled "not yet checked against a real file".
- **Cost vs expected for real accounts** (priority 1, same session, `docs/research/LOG.md` §U).
- **A data-freshness line on the dashboard** ("Data last synced 3 days ago ... Fills made since then are not in these numbers."), and a scheduled re-sync of accounts whose data is older than 24 hours.
- **Shadow mode for CCXT**: a way to compare the native Binance connector with CCXT field by field, before CCXT is trusted for Bybit and OKX history.

## Measured results

No new market measurement. The check reuses the account measurement job's quote rules and effective half-spread; its hand-computed fixture gives 2.0 bps against the mid and 16.0 bps of fees for the two test fills, and 0.04 and 0.32 in quote units (`ml-service/tests/csv_import/test_service.py`).

## What changed in the plan, and why

- **Funding costs are not built.** A key with a futures permission is refused by I3, so a stored key cannot read funding payments. Options and a recommendation: `docs/PROGRESS.md`, waiting item 16.
- **CCXT is a dev dependency, not a production one.** 70 MB and every exchange class do not belong in a 512 MB free instance. Shadow mode is a local script; the pure comparison is tested. Bybit and OKX via CCXT wait for a live shadow run (waiting item 17).
- **The re-sync is best effort.** The free instance sleeps; the stale-data line is what tells the truth.
- **Lint debt is partly left for M8** on purpose (the remaining warnings are structural, in screens M8 redesigns).
