# M2 — Real demos

**Status (2026-11-12):** built and verified offline. Three closing steps wait for Deepanshu (`docs/PROGRESS.md`, "Waiting for Deepanshu" items 7-10): the timing decision, the Atlas seed, then the re-lock and the live ablation re-record.

## What a user gets
- The three Binance demo traders are rebuilt from real public prints of January 2024. A market fill is a real print, at its price and time. A limit order rests at the price of the last real print on its side before it was placed. It fills at that limit price only when a later real print trades through it, at that print's time. An order is never filled at an invented price.
- The demos now have orders, so they show order shortfall, resting time and orders that never filled (19 cancelled orders across the three demos).
- The demos get a measured (v2) score from the same measurement job and scorer that real accounts use. Their published v1 grades stay as they are.

- **Whale study** (EXP-003). With only bursts before the fill counted, the share of "adverse" fills falls from 15.3% to 6.8%. That is no more than the opposite-side control (8.2%). It shows no association with the effective half-spread.

## What changed in the plan, and why
- **No v1 regrade of the new files** (`gradeDemoFiles.ts` was not built). The seed's v1 enrichment invents each trade's arrival price and spread with unseeded `Math.random()`, so a v1 grade of the new files would rest on invented numbers and could not be reproduced. The v1 grades stay as the frozen, published audits.
- **The production job and scorer are reused offline.** `measure_account` and `score_account_v2` take `data_source="synthetic-demo"`, for `demo-*` accounts only, on an in-memory database. The demo grades therefore come from the same code as real grades. The default stays real-user only.
- **demo-okx and demo-multi were not rebuilt.** www.okx.com was unreachable from this machine on 2026-11-12, and no official OKX fee page could be read. The proposal is to remove them, and to remove demo-bybit until Bybit fills are measured (decision 8). The Bybit tape loader is built and tested on a real fixture.
- **Measurement-v2 consequence for the demos.** A simulated fill keeps its real print's time. Fills taken from one public event therefore share a millisecond and are scored as one taker event (v2.1.0).
- **Fee snapshots of the demo accounts** are labelled `published-schedule`, never `account`. `FeeSnapshot.source` gains that value.

## Review (independent `reviewer` subagent on the M2 diff)
No high findings. The reviewer re-ran the whale study, and every EXP-003 number reproduced.
1. **(Medium) Seeding would blank demo panels.** The new trades have no per-trade v1 fields (`fillScore`, `slippageScore`, `arrivalPriceProxy`, `whale*`). Worst trades, cost attribution, the exchange comparison, the component means, recommendation regeneration and the whale panel read those fields. Meanwhile the locked v1 grade keeps showing. **Not applied, and on hold:** PROGRESS item 9 now says the seed waits until the demo dashboard shows the rebuilt demos honestly (an M2 closing task). No code path can recompute a demo's v1 grade: `/audit/run` is a POST, and demo accounts are read-only.
2. **(Medium) The seed had no rollback and no schema check.** Fixed:
   - The dry run validates every document against the Mongoose schemas (`validateWithModels`) and stops on any error.
   - The apply step inserts the new documents first and only then deletes the account's other documents (`mongoReplace`).
   - If the insert fails, the documents it inserted are removed, so the old ones stay.
   - The apply step prints the counts before it writes.
   - All of this is tested on mongodb-memory-server.
3. **(Low-medium) The demo fee snapshot's `fetchedAt` is not a fetch time, and the fee component is circular.**
   - The snapshot is dated at the window midpoint so that the 30-day rule covers the demo month. It carries the published schedule read on 2026-11-11.
   - The fee component scores 100 because the demo fees were generated at that same rate.
   - Recorded here and in LOG §L. The dashboard must label the demo fee component "not independently measured" (part of the closing task in finding 1).
4. **(Low) The ablation script.** Fixed:
   - It refuses to run on every arm and day if a target file exists.
   - It checks automatically, read-only, that the demos are seeded (demo-moderate has 245 orders).
   - The failure grep covers runs 4-6 only, without the bare `429`.
5. **(Low) EXP-003's spread statement overstated the evidence.** It is reworded as "no detectable difference at this resolution".
6. **(Low) The tape name check accepted a trailing newline.** It now uses `fullmatch`, with a test.
7. **(Low) A tape window entirely after the decision window raised an error.** It now returns an empty window, with a test.
8. **(Low) This report said every fill price is a real print.** Limit fills are at the limit, which is an earlier real print's price; the wording is fixed. Market orders can see a print in their own placement millisecond (`ts >= t`), while limit orders use `ts > t`. That is a one-millisecond asymmetry, recorded here.
9. **(Low) Defence in depth.** The order, order-measurement and fee-snapshot queries filter by `accountId` only, not `dataSource`. That is safe, because a real account and a demo account cannot share an id. Recorded, not changed.
10. **(Low) The review section was a placeholder.** This section replaces it.
