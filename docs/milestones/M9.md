## Claims audit

Method: a search of every frontend and backend text surface for advice, forward-looking, causal and compliance wording (the existing `findAdviceTerms` list plus "compliant", "guarantee", "save", "best execution", "alpha"), then a read of every hit.

- **Fixed:** the analytics heatmap said "Best execution window" and "You frequently perform poorly" (guidance and a habit, neither tested by the code); it now states the selected hour, the rule (at least 2 fills; average below 50 over at least 3) and "your past fills". The landing page described the OKX card as "Low fees" (an unverified claim about a third party; FillScore measures fees, it does not rate venues) and the hero line described only the v1 score. The README, ARCHITECTURE and SECURITY drift is corrected.
- **Left, on purpose:** the dashboard venue sentence ("Fills on X cost ~N bps less slippage than on Y in this period") is a past-tense, period-scoped statement from the v1 data and stays. The Council agent is named "Alpha Architect" in the UI; the name suggests alpha-seeking, so M8 (the design phase) should rename it.
- **Not found:** any use of "compliant" or similar in product copy; the legal text says "Not financial advice". The best-execution pack and its test were already worded "supports your documentation".
