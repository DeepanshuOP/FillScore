Passes 1 to 3 were the fix loop (the ordered maximum); passes 4 to 6 added the states the curated set needs (Council with the recorded demo run, onboarding refusal, light theme, phone menu) and re-checked the fixes.

- Pass 1 (the earlier crash did not recur): axe `aria-prohibited-attr` (SplitText `aria-label` on a paragraph, rank bars without a role), `label-content-name-mismatch` (account switcher, ⌘K), `button-name` (icon-only Method button at 390), 11 px horizontal overflow of the landing story at 390 (grid column sized by its widest scene), a crushed top bar at 1440, a clipped "All in" row in the hero card at 390, the sample-data banner squeezed at 390. All fixed; passes 2 and 3 clean.
- break-ui pass (`e2e-shots/break.ts`: worst-case data through the API mock, empty, one order, 320 px, 200 % zoom, keyboard only): the long client order id, a 14-character symbol, an 8-figure notional and a dust quantity hold; the empty states read well. Found and fixed: focus fell to `BODY` after the order drawer closed (now returns to the row), and "1 values cited" in the Council (the e2e matcher `values cited` became `values? cited`; the recorded run is unchanged).
- Motion review (landing timeline and drawer): reduced motion, skip, kill on unmount and the pre-JS state were already right. The sheet slid in with `ease-in-out`; it now uses an ease-out curve.
- Independent reviewer (subagent, 12 findings), disposition:
  - **Fixed:** no navigation in the marketing header at 390 (phone menu); page title cut to "Overvi…" at 390 (compact logo hidden below `sm`); mono source-field lines in the replay ledger and the story ledger (now a title and screen-reader text, per DESIGN §4); arrow glyphs on two links (DESIGN §9); the story's Fees tile now dashed and labelled "At the published rate, not measured", with "Synthetic trader, real prices" in the scene; Evidence pack cards aligned.
  - **Reviewer claim that was a fixture bug, fixed:** the Rankings panel showed about 10 bps under "Execution cost". The product ranks `shortfall.executionBps` (`routes/explain.ts`); the screenshot fixture ranked `totalBps` (execution plus fees). `cut-fixtures.ts` now ranks execution cost (SOLUSDT −0.50, ETHUSDT −0.02, BTCUSDT 0.00 bps). The product was right.
  - **Reviewer claim that was wrong:** "fee total does not match the visible fills". The order has three fills; their fees sum to $0.1125, the ledger's $0.11.
  - **Kept, with reason:** the Council sentence "disagreed on the cause of the costs" and the agent name "Fee Optimizer" are recorded model output and recorded agent names (rule 6: recorded output is never edited); to Deepanshu below. The drawer draws the order's fills, not the public prints: the replay payload carries the sources, not the prints, and rendering the full tape there needs a backend payload (a part of the next milestone, not a frontend fix). Tape call-outs in the hero: the reviewer reports two call-outs crossing the "placed" and drop lines and text of about 9 to 10 px (set in viewBox units); I did not measure this and did not change it, so it stays open.
