### Top 10 issues
1. **The 4a backfill overwrote the scorer's `spreadBps` on all 897 demo trades.** `ml-service/marketdata/enrich.py:77` writes `spreadBps` as `{cs, ar, n_pairs}`. The scorer reads it as a number (`backend/src/models/Trade.ts:38`, `types/index.ts:70`, `scoring/engine.ts:243`). Q1b: 897/897 are dicts. Consequences:
   - `scoreExchange()` throws for every trade, so a re-run of `executeAuditPipeline` skips every trade and fails with `no_trades_found` (`routes/audit.ts:61-79`).
   - `computeSpreadCost` now returns $0, because `isFinite({})` is false (`scoring/attribution.ts:127-136`).
   - The CSV spread column prints `[object Object]` (`routes/audit.ts:406`).
   - The 4a report and the test call `spreadBps` a "new" field (`ml-service/tests/test_backfill_markouts.py:93`, `REPORT_phase4a.md` §9). It was not new.
   - The original heuristic values are gone from Atlas, unless a backup exists (UNVERIFIED).
   - Running the backfill on any real account would make that account unscorable.
2. **The dashboard's COMPONENT SCORES cards are re-derived in the frontend with formulas the engine does not use** (`frontend/app/dashboard/page.tsx:700-733`):
   - SLIPPAGE = `100 − 6×avgSlippageBps`.
   - TIMING = a constant 65 or 85, chosen by `worstHour`.
   - SPREAD shows `avgFeeDragBps` labelled "Exchange spread cost". The "SPREAD 10.0 bps drag" users see is the 0.10% Binance fee, not a spread.
3. **The rolling demo accounts (demo-bybit/okx/multi, 190 trades) are simulated end to end, which breaks product rule 4.**
   - **Prices:** `40000 + Math.random()*5000` (BTC) and `2200 + Math.random()*300` (ETH), stamped with June–July 2026 timestamps (`backend/src/scripts/seedDatabase.ts:219-221, 280-282`).
   - **Arrival, VWAP and spread are random noise** (`seedDatabase.ts:23-40, 64-67`). They use unseeded `Math.random`, so a reseed changes the locked grades.
   - **Q2:** 100% of these fills sit outside the real 1 s bar, by a median of 3,224–4,184 bps.
   - **Q3:** their stored 60 s markouts have a median of about +3,450 to +3,676 bps, and the new Markouts card would show this.
4. **Every LIMIT fill of the three Binance demo users has slippage of exactly 0 by construction.** `whale/regen_fills.py:52-60` sets `arrivalPriceProxy = executionPrice`. Q1: 380/380 LIMIT fills show `exact0=100%`. So demo-disciplined's A grade carries a manufactured 100 slippage component. Q2: 96.6–98.8% of these LIMIT prices are outside the real 1 s bar at their timestamp, by a median of about 2 bps.
5. **Cost attribution v1 is wrong in three ways** (`scoring/attribution.ts`):
   - It uses `|arrival − exec|`, so price improvement counts as cost (`:76`).
   - It adds the full `spreadBps × notional` on top of arrival slippage, which double-counts the spread crossing and also charges maker fills (`:136, :167`).
   - It adds a flat 5 bps timing "cost" outside 08–16 UTC (`:49-53`), while the engine's window is 07–16 (`engine.ts:156`).
6. **The heuristic spread `(H−L)/mid × 0.15` on 1-minute bars** (`services/MarketDataService.ts:80-87`) overstates real spreads. On the same days, against the trade-sign spread from aggTrades (Q3):

   - SOL: 2.18 vs 1.05 bps (~2×).
   - BNB: 1.88 vs 3.16 bps, where it *understates*.

   This input drives 15% of the grade.

## 2. Deep dives

### 2.1 Demo-trade realism: DONE
- **Generation (Binance trio)** (`generateSyntheticTrades.ts:180-203`):
  - Fill = 1-min open × (1 ± `(0.0002+prng·0.0018)·slippageMult·spreadMult`) for market orders.
  - Fill = open × (1 ± 0.5–1.5 bps) for limit orders. A maker BUY therefore fills *above* the open.
  - The timestamp is a random second inside the minute.
  - The symbol multipliers BTC 1.0 / ETH 1.2 / BNB 1.5 / SOL 2.0 live here (`:190-193`), not in `estimateSpreadBps`.
- **Overwrites:**
  - `whale/regen_fills.py:52-115` re-prices MARKET fills from real aggTrades in [t, t+2 s]. It walks the quantity across prints but **ignores the aggressor side**. Arrival = the last print ≤ t.

### 2.4 OBI and spoofing: DONE
- **What data.binance.vision offers** (S3 listing, https://s3-ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com/data.binance.vision?delimiter=/&prefix=data/spot/daily/ and the matching `futures/um` prefixes):
  - Spot daily and monthly: `aggTrades`, `klines`, `trades` only. **No book data.**
  - USD-M daily: `aggTrades, bookDepth, bookTicker, indexPriceKlines, klines, markPriceKlines, metrics, premiumIndexKlines, trades`.
  - USD-M monthly: adds `fundingRate`.
- **Coverage (BTCUSDT):**
  - `bookDepth` daily runs 2023-01-01 → 2026-11-06.
  - `bookTicker` daily runs 2023-05-16 → **2024-03-30 (discontinued)**.
- **`bookDepth` format, verified on a downloaded file** (https://data.binance.vision/data/futures/um/daily/bookDepth/BTCUSDT/BTCUSDT-bookDepth-2024-01-15.zip): `timestamp, percentage, depth, notional`. Rows hold cumulative depth within ±1…±5% of price, at roughly 30 s snapshots. **These are aggregated bands, not L2 levels.** At ±1% they are about 4,000× coarser than the top-of-book levels an OBI uses.
- **Can we build a real historical OBI?**
  - Spot: **no**.
  - USD-M perps: only a coarse "futures depth-band imbalance". It is not top-5 OBI, and it is a different market from the spot fills being graded.
  - A spot OBI needs our own collector of `@depth` snapshots, forward only (ROADMAP T2.11 spec).
  - Spoofing (T2.14) needs sub-second L2 that we will never have historically.
- **Recommendation:** **DEFER** both. If anything is built, it is a collector-forward spot OBI, labelled with its start date. Never a synthetic OBI; never a futures-band number shown as spot OBI (ADR-0006, Proposed).
- **Cost:** L (collector infrastructure).

### 2.5 Execution latency: DONE
- **Timestamps received:**
  - Binance myTrades `time` = fill time (`TradeIngestionService.ts:93`). No order-creation time is fetched.
  - Bybit `execTime` = fill time (`BybitClient.ts:127`).
  - OKX `ts` = fill time (`OKXClient.ts:124`).
  - None of the connectors stores the order's creation or update time.
- **Measurable from read-only history:** with `allOrders` (Binance), `order/history` (Bybit) or `orders-history` (OKX) we could compute **order resting time** (placed → first fill) for limit orders. That is not latency. True exchange latency (client send → matching engine) needs client-side timestamps the user's own bot would have recorded.
- **Recommendation:** **REMOVE** "execution latency" from the plan. Optionally build "order resting time" (limit orders only) under that name (ADR-0007, Proposed).
- **Cost:** S (if built).

### 2.6 Whale pipeline: DONE
- **Definition:** a burst is consecutive same-direction aggTrades within ≤1 s whose summed notional is ≥ the threshold (`whale_score.py:28-34`, burst gap check at `~:135`).
- **Thresholds:** BTC/ETH $250k, SOL $100k, BNB $50k. They were calibrated to a 15–40% detection rate (ROADMAP §2.1; comment at `whale_score.py:32`), not to any market-impact criterion.
- **Weighting:** decay τ = 10 s (`:34, :156`) over a ±30 s aggTrades window (`regen_fills.py:66`; ROADMAP T2.12).
- **Adverse sign:** adverse = whale pressure in the *same* direction as the user's side (`:165-166`).
- **Real public data?** Yes, aggTrades from data.binance.vision (`whale/aggtrades_window.py`).
- **Flaw:** `abs(seconds_from_trade)` (`:156`) weights bursts *after* the fill equally. A burst 10 s after a buy is counted as having "hurt" that buy, which contradicts the comment at `:162`. Restrict "adverse" to t ≤ 0, or rename the flag "coincided with same-direction flow".
- **Downstream honesty:**
  - Copy uses "coincided" / "concurrent".
  - The claim check bans causal connectives (LOG §A).
  - grep found no surviving "caused", "7.52" or "p=0.021" in the README, frontend, scoring or prompts. ROADMAP §2.1 keeps them only as retired.
- **Remaining issue:** the slippage the whale flag was tested against is synthetic, or 0 by construction for LIMIT fills. So any whale ↔ slippage statistic is still uninterpretable (ROADMAP §2.3.1 is right).
- **Cost:** S (pre-fill fix); the re-run waits on real or realistic fills.
