# FillScore research log

Findings, decisions and numbers, by topic. **Every number comes from a phase report or a persisted run.** Phase tags are in brackets.

## A. System design (decisions worth citing)
- **Placeholder grounding.** LLMs write `{{namespace.key}}` placeholders, and only the renderer inserts numbers, using one formatter (`render.format_value`). Stray numerals lead to one regeneration, then sentence redaction, then a code-built fallback sentence. [1c–1d]
- **Claim check.** Advice terms are always flagged. Counterfactual terms ("avoidable", "saved"…) are allowed only for an action that passed verification. Causal connectives ("due to", "driven by"…) and mechanism words (manipulation, spoof, exogenous…) are strict on debate text. "Whale" is allowed only beside the rendered whale-adverse count when that count is above 0. [2a, 2c, 2c.2, 3a]
- **Counterfactual verifier.** Replays the trader's own trades under an action (maker instead of taker, excluding hours, venue shift, smaller clips). It passes only if `effect_usd > max($1, 0.5% of fees)`, plus duplicate and contradiction checks, one forced re-reason, then drop. [1c]
- **Deterministic duplicate strip.** A raw numeral equal to the adjacent placeholder's rendered value is removed before grounding, which fixed gpt-oss-20b writing "42.6% … {{key}}". [2c]
- **Eval invariants** (mutation-tested; each bad output fails only its own invariant): `rating_allowed`, `evidence_cited`, `no_flagged_terms`, `numerals_grounded` (exact match to the formatter; a random-integer probe passes only 1.5–4.5%, down from about 45% before), `findings_gated`, `packet_provenance`. [3b, 3b.2]

- **Rating agreement across runs:** single 9/9 vs Council 5/9.

## C. Quant results
- **Equal fees:** Binance and Bybit base tiers charge 0.10%/0.10%, so a fee-only maker counterfactual is exactly $0 (`test_gate_zero_findings.py`). OKX is 0.08%/0.10%. [3a]
- **Market data choice:** data.binance.vision daily **1s klines**, which are 5–20× lighter than aggTrades (BTCUSDT: 1s klines about 2–3 MB/day, against 6–65 MB/day for aggTrades). Spot data from 2025-01-01 has microsecond timestamps. The BTCUSDT file checked was dense: 86,400 rows, every gap 1 s. [4a]
- **Markout:** `side_sign × (P(t+h) − fill)/fill × 1e4`, where positive means the price moved in the trader's favour. [4a]
  - Horizons: 1, 5, 30, 60 and 300 s.
  - P(t+h) is the close of the last 1s bar that had ended by floor(t+h), so there is no look-ahead.
  - Values staler than 5 s are null; missing values are excluded, not zeroed.
- **Spread estimators** on 1-minute bars over ±30 min, with at least 10 minute-pairs: [4a]
  - Corwin–Schultz (2012, J. Finance 67(2)) and Abdi–Ranaldo (2017, RFS 30(12));
  - the conservative (smaller) estimate is used; negatives are floored at 0;
  - biases: built for daily equities, and inflated by volatility.
- **Spread-aware maker counterfactual:** `net = N·(taker−maker) + N·min(halfSpread, 1.0 bp) − adverse`, and `effect = 0.5 × Σnet` (fill-probability haircut). Adverse selection comes from the trader's own maker-fill 60 s markouts when there are at least 10 maker fills; otherwise it falls back to their taker markouts, floored at the spread credit. [4a]
- **Demo counterfactual results** (offline, real public market data, synthetic trades): no account clears the $1 threshold. [4a]
  - demo-moderate at 0.8: +$0.155.
  - demo-multi at 0.8: +$0.415.
  - **demo-aggressive: −$0.385 and −$1.061.** Its own maker fills suffered 1.53 bps of adverse selection at 60 s, more than the spread credit, so more passive execution would not have lowered modelled cost.
- **Backfill dry-run on the demo trades:** 897/897 trades priced, 0 fetch errors, a 394 MB cache. On BNBUSDT samples, Corwin–Schultz gave 2.2–3.6 bps and Abdi–Ranaldo 2.1–3.2 bps, so half-spreads came out at 1.05–1.58 bps. These **exceed the 1.0 bp cap**, and likely overstate BNBUSDT's real quoted spread (about one tick, ~0.3 bps at $310). Bar-range estimators read high on liquid crypto; this motivates a trade-sign estimator from aggTrades. [4a follow-up, 2026-11-07]
- **Open assumptions:** the 1.0 bp half-spread cap; the 0.5 fill haircut; Bybit/OKX fills priced against Binance spot.

## G. Milestone 1, Part A: measurement layer on real prints [M1, 2026-11-11]
Code: `ml-service/measure/`. Tests: `ml-service/tests/measure/` (89 tests). Fixtures: `ml-service/tests/fixtures/market/SOURCE.md`. Method page (generated): `docs/methodology/measurement-v1.0.0.md`.

- **Definitions.** The mid comes from aggressor-side prints strictly before the fill (`ts < t`): the ask proxy is the last buyer-initiated print, and the bid proxy is the last seller-initiated print. Each proxy can be at most 5,000 ms old, and the mid needs ask ≥ bid; if those fail, the last print is used (`last_trade`), and if nothing is fresh, the mid is `None`. Effective half-spread = D(p − m)/m; realized(h) = D(p − m_h)/m; impact(h) = D(m_h − m)/m; all × 1e4, h ∈ {1, 15, 60, 300} s. The identity effective = realized + impact is checked at runtime for every horizon.
- **Timestamp unit finding.** data.binance.vision spot files use milliseconds up to 2024 and microseconds in 2025 (verified on BTCUSDT 2024-01-15 and 2025-03-04). The loader detects the unit per file, rejects a file that mixes units, and floors microseconds to milliseconds.
- **Real-slice numbers (offline test `test_measure_fill_real_btc_slice`).** BTCUSDT 2024-01-15, pretend buy = aggregate trade 2830846703 at 42864.25, t = 1705327260000. Ask proxy 42864.25 and bid proxy 42864.24, both 2 ms old. Mid 42864.245; effective half-spread 0.005/42864.245 × 1e4 = 0.0011665 bps (half of one 0.01 tick); E/Q = 1.0. Mids at +1/+15/+60 s are 42864.245, 42841.375 and 42824.715, so the 15 s impact is (42841.375 − 42864.245)/42864.245 × 1e4 = −5.3354 bps. +300 s falls outside the 2-minute slice, so it is `None`. BNBUSDT the same day: pretend buy at 318.90 (aggregate trade 518231524), mid 318.85, effective 1.56814 bps (half of the 0.1 tick). Command: `cd ml-service && .venv/bin/pytest -q tests/measure/test_enrich_fill.py`.
- **Limitation.** Prints are a proxy for quotes: when the price moves between the two proxy prints, ask − bid overstates the spread. The proxy ages are stored with every measurement (`quoteAgesMs`) so this can be filtered or reported. A pretend fill at its own print shows the floor: on liquid pairs the measured effective half-spread is half a tick.
- **Defects the tests found.** The price step kept float noise at 12 decimals (now 8, the exchange precision). The header-row skip dropped a data row.
- **Plan correction.** The 4R-1a plan said horizon +1 s is `None` on fixture W. It is not: at t = 3500 the ask proxy 100.10 (age 1,000 ms) and the bid proxy 100.00 (age 2,500 ms) are both fresh, so the mid is 100.05. Only +60 s and +300 s are `None`.
- **Flaky test check.** `tests/test_llm_router.py::test_schema_mismatch_records_loc_and_type_only` passed 30 of 30 runs in isolation, and it passed in the full suite (949 passed, 1 skipped). No failure was reproduced, so no fix was made.

## H. Milestone 1, Part B: orders, fees, order-level shortfall, the measurement job [M1, 2026-11-11]
Decision record: `docs/research/decisions/ADR-0011-measurement-job-outbox-and-service-token.md`.

- **Exchange facts (docs read 2026-11-11 through the docs-research subagent; cited in the code).** Binance `GET /api/v3/allOrders`: weight 20, `symbol` required, `limit` max 1000, `orderId` returns orders with id ≥ orderId, startTime/endTime span ≤ 24 h, times in ms; some historical orders report a negative `cummulativeQuoteQty` (data unavailable) [developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/rest-api/account-endpoints]. `GET /api/v3/account/commission`: weight 20; standard, tax and special commissions each with maker, taker, buyer and seller parts, plus a discount block [same page]. Order statuses: NEW, PENDING_NEW, PARTIALLY_FILLED, FILLED, CANCELED, PENDING_CANCEL, REJECTED, EXPIRED, EXPIRED_IN_MATCH [.../enums]. Bybit v5 order history: limit 1–50, a time span of at most 7 days, cursor paging; older than 7 days only orders with fills [bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/order/order-list]. OKX order-history details could not be confirmed from the official page (it came back truncated), so OKX order sync is not built yet.
- **Commission formula (Binance Commission FAQ, developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/faqs/commission_faq).** rate = (standard[liquidity] + standard[side]) × discount + tax[liquidity] + tax[side] + special[liquidity] + special[side]; the discount applies only to the standard part, and only when the account and the symbol have it enabled and the fee is paid in BNB. The FAQ's worked example (taker sell of 17,491.25 USDT notional, BNB at 260 USDT, discount 0.25) gives rate 0.06000245 and 4.036607129 BNB; `tests/measure/test_fees.py` reproduces it exactly.
- **Order-level implementation shortfall (Perold 1988).** Hand case (buy 2.0, arrival 100.00, fills 1.0 at 100.02 with mid 100.01 and 0.5 at 100.04 with mid 100.03, end mid 100.10, fees 0.15): execution 2.0, spread 0.75, impact/timing 1.25, opportunity 2.5, fees 7.5, total 12.0 bps. The same values come out of the full job on a synthetic tape built to reproduce them (`tests/measure/test_service.py`). Orders that never filled get a pure opportunity cost (buy 2.0, arrival 100.00, cancelled at 100.10: 10.0 bps), which covers the "cost of orders that didn't fill".
- **Rules that avoid made-up numbers.** A fill without a fresh mid leaves the order's spread and impact split as `None` (its execution cost is still measured). Fees in a non-USD quote currency leave fees and total as `None`. A failed commission fetch stores nothing, never an assumed rate. Fills younger than 305 s wait for their last horizon.
- **Limitations (Part B).** Only Binance fills are measured (the prints come from Binance). The commission snapshot is the rate at fetch time, not necessarily the rate at an older trade. 1 USDT, USDC or FDUSD is taken as 1 USD when fees in USD are compared with a quote-currency notional.

## L. M2: the three Binance demos rebuilt from real prints, first v2 grades [M2, 2026-11-12]
- **Command:** `cd ml-service && .venv/bin/python scripts/build_demo_v2.py` (about 6 minutes). Market orders take the next own-side aggressor prints for up to 5 minutes; limit orders rest at the same-side proxy and fill only when a print trades through them, within 30 minutes. Fees: Binance regular tier 0.1% (fee page searched 2026-11-11; January 2024 not separately confirmed). Output: `ml-service/demo_v2/out/`. Nothing was written to MongoDB: measurement and scoring ran through the production job and scorer on an in-memory database (`data_source="synthetic-demo"`).
- **Counts.** aggressive: 311 orders (309 filled, 2 cancelled), 721 fills, 2 limit decisions skipped (no fresh same-side print within 5 s); moderate: 245 orders (234 / 11), 607 fills, 3 skipped; disciplined: 144 orders (138 / 6), 385 fills, 1 skipped. - **Grades, v1 locked vs v2.1.0 (offline, `demo_v2/out/grades_v2.json`):**

  | Demo | v1 locked | v2 composite (95% band) | slippage | fees | timing | spread |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | demo-aggressive | 60.67 C | 78.26 B (77.31-79.18) | 76.28 | 100 | 51.92 | 90.55 |

- **Finding: the order is reversed, and timing drives it.** Spread (90-92) and slippage (75-77, the market median by construction) barely separate the three profiles; fees are 100 for all three (the demo account pays exactly the published rate it is compared with). Timing separates them. The disciplined profile trades 08:00-15:00 UTC, which includes 13:00-15:00, the most expensive hours of every v2.1.0 hour profile; the aggressive profile trades 21:00-03:00 UTC, mostly among the cheapest (21:00 is the cheapest BTC hour; 00:00-01:00 are mid-range). The hour profile is the median quoted half-spread plus the median absolute 1-minute move, and the move dominates (for BTC, about 1.5-3.5 bps of move against under 0.001 bps of half-spread). So the timing component mostly ranks hours by volatility. Busy hours are also the most liquid, so "costlier hour" here means "more volatile hour", not "worse execution". This is spec decision D5, applied as approved; it is listed for Deepanshu as a design question, and nothing was changed.
- **Limitations.** The decisions are synthetic behaviour (provenance `synthetic-demo`); only the prices are real. The limit rule is conservative (a touch never fills), which favours adverse selection: a resting buy fills only after the price fell through it. The hour profiles come from August-November 2026, and the demo trades are from January 2024. demo-okx and demo-multi were not rebuilt: www.okx.com was unreachable from this machine on 2026-11-12, and no official OKX fee page could be read. demo-bybit fills would read "not measured" under v2, because the measurement job measures Binance fills only.
